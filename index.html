Sensory Modulation Screener

Burnout Reduction Workshop · Primary Care Providers

This 5-minute screener helps you identify how your nervous system engages with clinical environments — and where small changes might meaningfully reduce your daily stress. There are no right or wrong answers. Your results are private and stay on your device.

You'll be asked about your responses to sound, light, touch, movement, and cognitive load — both in general and in clinical settings. At the end you'll get a personal profile with concrete suggestions.
